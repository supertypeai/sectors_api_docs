---
title: "SGX Company Report API: Singapore Stocks"
openapi: GET /v2/sgx/company/report/{symbol}/
sidebarTitle: Full company report for an SGX-listed symbol
description: Build an SGX company report for any Singapore-listed stock, including its directors, its major shareholders and how its valuation compares with peers.
---

<Note>SGX symbol: 3-4 characters (letters or digits), optionally followed by `.SI` (case-insensitive) on input. E.g. `D05`, `U11`, `Z74`. Output always carries the `.SI` suffix.</Note>

Use `sections` to fetch only the data you need and reduce response size.

<Accordion title="Available sections">
- **overview**: Market cap, volume, sector, sub-sector, price changes (1d/7d/1m/1y/3y/ytd), all-time price highs/lows, listing date, registered address and website
- **valuation**: PE, PS, PCF, PB ratios, historical yearly valuation and peer averages
- **financials**: Historical revenue and earnings by year, EPS along with its yearly history and growth, margins, ratios
- **dividend**: Dividend yield, growth rate, payout ratio, historical dividends and any announced upcoming dividends
- **management**: Current directors and executives with their position, age and appointment date, together with former officers and the date they stepped down
- **ownership**: Major shareholders with their share count, ownership percentage and holding value in SGD
</Accordion>

<Info>Costs 1 API credit per requested section. Default behavior (all 6 sections) consumes 6 credits.</Info>
