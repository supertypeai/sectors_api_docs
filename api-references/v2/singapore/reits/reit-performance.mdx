---
title: "SGX REIT Performance API: DPU, Gearing"
openapi: GET /v2/sgx/reits/performance/{symbol}/
sidebarTitle: SGX REIT Performance
description: Compare a Singapore REIT's DPU, gearing and interest cover year by year to see whether its distributions are growing and its interest is still well covered.
---

`market_cap`, `last_close_price`, `distribution_yield` and `price_to_nav` are today's numbers, so they stay the same whichever `financial_year` returned. Distribution yield is the latest distribution per unit divided by today's price, and price to NAV is today's price divided by the latest NAV per unit.

<Note>REITs close their books in different months, so one REIT's FY2025 can end in March and another's in December. `period_end` gives the exact date. `properties_location` is written the way the REIT reports it. Money is in SGD, and ratios are fractions (0.4 means 40%).</Note>
