---
title: "SGX REIT Properties API: Portfolio List"
openapi: GET /v2/sgx/reits/properties/{symbol}/
sidebarTitle: SGX REIT Properties
description: List every property a Singapore REIT reports for a financial year, ranked by valuation, with revenue and status, including properties sold or for sale.
---

Every property one SGX REIT reported for a financial year, from the highest valuation down. Properties that were sold or are up for sale stay in the list, marked in `status`.

<Note>Valuation and revenue cover the whole property, even when the REIT owns only part of it. Multiply by `ownership` to get the REIT's share; `null` means unreported. Areas are in square metres, and `lease_term_years` is the original lease length, not what's left. An active property at 0% occupancy is usually being redeveloped or built.</Note>

<Info>Costs 1 API credit.</Info>
