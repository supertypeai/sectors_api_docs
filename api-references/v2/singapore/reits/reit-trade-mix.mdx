How one SGX REIT's rental income splits by the kind of business its tenants run, such as Food & Beverages or Financial & Professional Services, for a financial year. Biggest share first.

<Note>`pct` is a share of the measure named in `pct_basis`, usually gross rental income. A few REITs split their mix by segment (`basis_segment`), and they don't add up the same way: for some, each segment adds up to 100% on its own; for others, all segments together add up to 100%. So only compare `pct` within the same REIT and segment.</Note>

<Info>Costs 1 API credit.</Info>
