---
title: "SGX REIT Profile API: Sponsor & Manager"
openapi: GET /v2/sgx/reits/profile/{symbol}/
sidebarTitle: SGX REIT Profile
description: Check who stands behind a Singapore REIT and who runs it day to day, to compare sponsor backing and governance across S-REITs.
---

Covers who runs the REIT (sponsor, trustee, REIT manager, property manager, and the master lessee or operator if it has one) and the board of directors of the REIT manager.

<Info>Costs 1 API credit.</Info>
