---
title: "SGX Stock News API: Singapore Market News"
openapi: GET /v2/sgx/news/
sidebarTitle: SGX News
description: Feed SGX stock news into your app or AI agent, filtered to the Singapore companies, sectors or topic tags your users follow.
---

Results are paginated and support filtering by sector, sub-sector, tags, symbols, and date range.

<Note>SGX symbol: 3-4 characters (letters or digits). E.g. `D05`, `U11`, `Z74`, `TCPD`. Output always carries the `.SI` suffix.</Note>

<Note>Date filters: both `start` and `end` are independent and optional — omit either side to leave that bound unconstrained. Future `end` dates return 400.</Note>

<Info>Costs 1 API credit.</Info>
