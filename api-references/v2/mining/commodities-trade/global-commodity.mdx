---
title: Global Commodity Production & Reserves API
openapi: GET /v2/mining/global-commodity/
sidebarTitle: Global Commodity Data
description: Compare Indonesia's coal, nickel, gold, copper and bauxite production and reserves with other producing countries in global commodity data.
---

At least one of `commodity_type` or `country` must be provided.

<Note>Available `commodity_type` values: `Coal`, `Gold`, `Nickel`, `Copper`, `Bauxite`.</Note>

`production_volume` is in the row's `production_volume_unit`: `Mt` for coal and bauxite, `kt` for nickel and copper, `t` for gold. Nickel and copper are **contained metal** from mine production, so nickel here is not comparable with [Total Commodity Production](https://docs.sectors.app/api-references/v2/mining/sites-production/commodity-production), which reports nickel ore.

`resources_reserves`, `resources_reserves_share` and `export_import_usd` are only filled for coal, and not for every coal country. Any field without data is `null`.

<Info>Costs 1 API credit.</Info>
