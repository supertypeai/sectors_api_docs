---
title: "Commodity Price History API: Coal, Nickel"
openapi: GET /v2/mining/commodities/{commodity_name}/price/
sidebarTitle: Commodity Price History
description: Chart coal and nickel price history alongside gold, copper and other metals to follow the commodities Indonesian mining companies produce.
---

Data is monthly (bi-weekly for recent Coal entries).

<Note>Use [List Commodities](https://docs.sectors.app/api-references/v2/mining/commodities-trade/commodities) to discover all available commodity names.</Note>

<Warning>Requesting more than 3 years will return a 400 error.</Warning>

<Info>Costs 1 API credit.</Info>
