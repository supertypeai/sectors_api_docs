---
title: Indonesia Coal & Nickel Production API
openapi: GET /v2/mining/total-production/
sidebarTitle: Total Commodity Production
description: Follow Indonesia's national coal, nickel, gold and copper production over time to gauge supply growth and spot the years output rose or fell.
---

Results include all years, ordered by year descending.

<Note>Available `commodity_type` values: `Coal`, `Nickel`, `Gold`, `Copper`.</Note>

What each commodity measures (Indonesia, from BPS):

| `commodity_type` | `unit` | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Coal` | `Mt` | Coal mined |
| `Nickel` | `Mt` | Nickel **ore** mined, not contained nickel |
| `Copper` | `kt` | Copper **concentrate**, not contained copper |
| `Gold` | `t` | Gold produced |

Nickel here is ore tonnage, so it is not comparable with [Global Commodity Data](https://docs.sectors.app/api-references/v2/mining/commodities-trade/global-commodity), which reports contained nickel.

BPS publishes no 2016 figures for copper, gold or nickel, so those years are absent and 2017's `prev_year_volume` and `yoy_change_percent` are `null`.

<Info>Costs 1 API credit.</Info>
