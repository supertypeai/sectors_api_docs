---
title: Mining Production & Reserves API by Company
openapi: GET /v2/mining/companies/performance/{slug}/
sidebarTitle: Mining Company Performance
description: Benchmark the production and reserve depth of individual Indonesian mining companies year by year, and compare coal miners' strip ratios with their peers.
---

When `year` is omitted, data for the latest available year is returned.

Production and sales volumes are in the unit given by `commodity_stats.unit`. Keys inside `resources_reserves` carry their unit as a suffix:

- `_Mt`: million tonnes (ore, or contained copper for `Cu_*_Mt`)
- `_wmt` / `_dmt` (nickel ore): **million** wet / dry metric tonnes
- `_Kt` (contained nickel and cobalt): thousand tonnes
- `_koz`: thousand troy ounces
- `_g_per_ton`: grams per tonne; `_pct`: percent

<Info>Costs 1 API credit.</Info>
